The history of Nepal is rich and diverse, shaped by many cultures, kingdoms, and traditions over thousands of years. In ancient times, the region was ruled by the Kirat dynasty, followed by the Licchavi period, which brought development in art, architecture, and governance. Later, during the Malla period, Nepal saw the growth of beautiful cities like Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, and Patan, known for their temples and culture. In the 18th century, Prithvi Narayan Shah unified the small kingdoms into one nation, forming modern Nepal. After this, the country went through the Rana rule, where prime ministers held most of the power, limiting the king’s authority. In 1951, democracy was introduced, although Nepal faced many political changes afterward, including becoming a republic in 2008. Today, Nepal is known for its cultural diversity, history, and natural beauty, including the famous Mount Everest
